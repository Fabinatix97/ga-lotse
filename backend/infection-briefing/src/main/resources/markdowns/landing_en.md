A certificate will be issued confirming that the training has been completed. This certificate must be presented to the employer and must not be more than three months old when the employee starts work for the first time. Anyone who already has a certificate can present it directly to their employer and will receive training when they start work. It is not necessary to undergo initial training again at the public health department!

## Requirements
The prerequisite for instruction in accordance with § 43 IfSG at the health department in Frankfurt am Main is that your registered place of residence or your workplace (your employer's business premises/place of work) is located in Frankfurt. If your registered place of residence is outside Frankfurt, we require a certificate from your employer stating that you are or will be employed in Frankfurt.

## Procedure
Appointments can be made online. Initial instruction is only available by prior appointment.
Please arrive at the health department five minutes before your appointment and register at the reception desk. The appointment takes about 30 minutes. You will receive an instruction text to read through and watch a short film. You will then receive your certificate.

## School internships or volunteer work
A reduced fee of €10.00 is charged for compulsory, unpaid school internships and volunteer work. Please bring a certificate from the school confirming the school internship or, in the case of volunteer work, a certificate from the place of employment. Students must be able to identify themselves with a valid student ID and their identity card/passport. For minors, the aforementioned regulation regarding parental consent applies.

## Required documents
- Identity card/passport
- If you are not registered in Frankfurt: Proof from your employer of your place of work in Frankfurt

## Costs
A fee of currently €29.00 is charged for conducting the instruction and issuing the certificate, and a fee of currently €12.00 is charged for issuing a duplicate (duplicates are available for up to 10 years after the initial instruction). Payment can be made in cash or with a German Girocard.
Please present a valid identity card/passport. Minors up to and including the age of 15 must be accompanied by a legal guardian; from the age of 16, a written declaration of consent is sufficient.
