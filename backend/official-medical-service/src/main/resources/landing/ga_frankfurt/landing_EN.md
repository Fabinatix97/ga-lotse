**Please arrive on the day of the examination on an empty stomach. In other words, please do not eat anything for 3 - 4 hours before the medical examination and only consume a small amount of unsweetened drinks (e.g. water, tea).**

**Bring the following documents with you to your medical examination:**
- ID Card / Passport
- Completed medical history form (see download box)
- Letter of assignment (letter from the employer stating the reason for the examination) with a copy
- Copies of medical documents (e.g. hospital discharge reports, certificates, etc.)
- Proof of currently prescribed medications (when applicable)

**For certificates of exam eligibility, please either bring or consider the following:**
- We are only responsible for students in Frankfurt, except for the 2nd state law examination, where the responsibility depends on the place of residence (consultation with the examination office is required in advance)
- Please arrive for exams no later than the day of the exam (even without an appointment) or when submitting an academic paper after making an appointment by telephone

**Please bring the following originals to your appointment or upload in advance:**
- Medical documents (e.g. specialist reports, hospital discharge reports, X-rays, CT/MRI findings)
- Certificate from your university or college regarding the examination date.
- Student- or Personal ID, or Passport
- Fee of 50 euros (payable with cash or debit card).

**Examination (in)ability / extension of deadlines:**
- In the event of illness, the examination regulations (schools, colleges, universities and similar institutions) stipulate that it is possible to withdraw from the examination or that a submission deadline can be extended in the event of health impairments. The respective examination board decides whether a medical certificate is required
- Before visiting the public health department, a medical certificate from a family doctor or specialist must be obtained, stating the diagnosis(es) and therapy
