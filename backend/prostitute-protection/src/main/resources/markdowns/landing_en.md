# Health Consultation for Sex Workers

The Prostituiertenschutzgesetz (ProstSchG) "Act on the Regulation of the Prostitution Trade and the Protection of Persons Working in Prostitution" was enforce on July 1, 2017. The law aims to better protect those working in prostitution, strengthen their right to self-determination, promote their health protection, create better working conditions, and combat crime in prostitution such as human trafficking or violence.

## Who must attend health consultations and where does this take place?

All persons working in prostitution must attend health consultations at the public health department (Gesundheitsamt). Sex workers are defined by the law as persons who provide erotic or sexual services (e.g., also erotic massage or escort services) in exchange for payment.

## Why must one attend health consultations?

Health consultations are intended to ensure that all sex workers have access to essential information on health protection. Depending on the situation of the person being counseled, the consultation may address questions about preventing sexually transmitted diseases, maternity protection, or risks of drug use.

Health counseling is a requirement mandated by law as a prerequisite for registration in the municipality where one primarily works.

## What is an alias certificate?

The alias certificate is an additional certificate with a fictitious stage name or working name. The alias certificate serves to protect the personal data of sex workers.

## How often must health counseling take place?

- For persons over 21 years of age:
  - Follow-up counseling must be attended at least every twelve months.
- For persons between 18 and 21 years of age:
  - Follow-up counseling must be attended at least every six months.
